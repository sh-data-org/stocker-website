## The next US market holidays

**The next full US stock market holiday is Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, 2026. On Friday, November 27, the regular stock session closes early at 1 p.m. Eastern.** Christmas Day, December 25, is also closed; Christmas Eve, December 24, has a 1 p.m. Eastern early close.

This calendar was checked November 9, 2026. It covers scheduled regular equity sessions, not every options, bond, futures or broker-specific session. Confirm your exchange and broker's current notices before placing an order.

## Early closes in 2026 and 2027

| Date                        | Regular equity session closes |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Friday, November 27, 2026   | 1 p.m. Eastern                |
| Thursday, December 24, 2026 | 1 p.m. Eastern                |
| Friday, November 26, 2027   | 1 p.m. Eastern                |

An early close is a shortened trading day, not a full-day closure. Options and extended sessions can have different hours. In particular, December 24, 2027 is a full closure for the observed Christmas holiday, rather than an early-close session.

## Regular hours and time zones

The NYSE core stock session runs from **9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Eastern Time** on a normal trading day. Eastern Time follows US daylight saving changes; it is not a fixed UTC offset throughout the year.

If you follow US companies from another country, use the event's named time zone in your calendar. A US evening earnings call can fall on the next date locally. A bank holiday in your country also does not establish whether a US stock exchange is open.

## Is the stock market open on Veterans Day 2026?

Yes. Wednesday, November 11 is not a closure in the published 2026 equity holiday schedule. Do not apply a bond-market or bank holiday schedule to stocks automatically.

## Is the stock market open on Black Friday?

The regular stock session is scheduled to open on November 27, 2026 and November 26, 2027, with an early close at 1 p.m. Eastern. Thanksgiving itself is closed in both years.
