**Entergy (ETR), Constellation Energy (CEG) and Eaton (ETN)** give you three different ways to research data center power. Entergy is a regulated utility that builds and operates infrastructure to serve customers. Constellation generates electricity and signs contracts to sell it. Eaton supplies electrical equipment that helps route and protect that power.

| Company                 | What the public record shows                                                                                             | What to check in 2027                                                                          |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Entergy (ETR)**       | Louisiana regulators approved infrastructure for Meta’s original data center project.                                    | Look for construction progress and revised cost or in-service estimates on that approved work. |
| **Constellation (CEG)** | Microsoft signed a 20-year agreement tied to the restart of a Pennsylvania nuclear unit.                                 | Look for regulatory approvals and confirmation of the restart schedule.                        |
| **Eaton (ETN)**         | Its electrical business reported more than 100% year-over-year growth in rolling 12-month data center orders in Q3 2026. | See whether sales keep growing as Eaton ships the gear and whether segment margins hold.       |

## Entergy builds the infrastructure a site needs

A large campus needs a grid connection and enough dependable generation to serve it. The Louisiana Public Service Commission approved generation and transmission investment for Meta’s original Richland Parish data center in 2025. That approval is a concrete milestone. To follow the economics, look in Entergy’s filings for construction spending, the costs it expects to recover from the customer, and the date each phase is expected to enter service. [Entergy’s approval announcement](https://www.entergy.com/news/entergy-louisiana-receives-lpsc-approval-for-major-infrastructure-investments-to-support-metas-data-center-and-improve-reliability) · [Entergy’s September 2026 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/65984/000006598426000325/etr-20260930.htm)

## Constellation has a signed customer and a restart to deliver

Constellation announced a **20-year power purchase agreement with Microsoft** in September 2024. It plans to restart the former Three Mile Island Unit 1 in Pennsylvania as the Crane Clean Energy Center. The project page lists **835 megawatts** of capacity and expects the plant to go online in **2027**. A signed buyer gives the project a commercial basis. The timeline still depends on regulatory approvals, refurbishment and bringing the unit back into service. [Microsoft agreement announcement](https://investors.constellationenergy.com/node/8711/pdf) · [Crane project update](https://www.constellationenergy.com/about/locations/crane-clean-energy-center.html)

## Eaton sells the electrical equipment

Eaton makes equipment that distributes, protects and manages power, including switchgear and backup power systems. In its Q3 2026 presentation, Eaton said electrical-sector data center orders more than doubled on a rolling 12-month basis, while its data center revenue grew approximately **65% from Q3 2025**. The two figures cover different periods and stages of the sale. In the next filing, check whether orders are turning into shipments and whether higher volumes are supporting electrical segment margins. [Eaton Q3 2026 presentation](https://www.eaton.com/content/dam/eaton/company/investor-relations/quarterly-earnings/filings/2026/q3/q3-2026-analyst-presentation.pdf)

Record the original announcement or filing, the milestone and the date you’ll revisit it. A utility project could be progressing if construction stays on schedule. A generator needs its plant online to deliver contracted power. An equipment supplier needs to ship its orders and collect payment.
