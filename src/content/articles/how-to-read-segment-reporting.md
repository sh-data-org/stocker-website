## Find the note before comparing divisions

Search the filing for segment reporting or reportable segments. The note explains how management groups the business. The labels may describe geographies, business units or a combination.

## Use Costco as a geography example

Costco’s FY2026 segment note reports the United States, Canada and Other International. The U.S. segment reported $219.823 billion of revenue against $303.154 billion consolidated. That is about 72.5% of the consolidated figure.

## Check the definition of segment profit

A segment metric may differ from the company’s net income. Read the footnote and any reconciliation before adding segment figures or comparing margins across businesses. Confirm that the numerator and denominator refer to the same segment and year.

## Write a question the table can answer

Ask which geography contributed most to the change in operating income, then compare the columns. If a company changes how it reports segments, check for restated comparison periods. Keep the note and period in your [research worksheet](/tools/research-template/).
