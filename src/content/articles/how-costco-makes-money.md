## Start with two revenue lines

Costco’s consolidated income statement separates net sales from membership fee revenue. For the fiscal year ended August 30, 2026, it reported $297.247 billion in net sales and $5.907 billion in membership fees. Together they produce $303.154 billion of total revenue.

## Revenue and profit answer different questions

Fees were about 1.95% of total revenue. That ratio describes the revenue mix. It cannot tell you the standalone profit from memberships because the income statement presents expenses for the broader business.

## Check what changed

Compare the membership and merchandise lines with the prior 52-week year. Costco’s filing says higher membership fees reflected factors including member sign-ups and fee increases. Look in management’s discussion for the drivers before interpreting the growth rate.

## Add one question to your worksheet

Try: if fee increases account for part of growth, how will I track member renewals in later periods? Record the fiscal end date, the original filing and the exact metric. Use the [free research worksheet](/tools/research-template/) to keep the evidence with the question.
