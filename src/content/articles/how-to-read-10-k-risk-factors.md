## Start with Item 1A

The SEC’s guide points readers to Item 1A for significant risks a company identifies. Choose a risk connected to how the company earns revenue, uses cash or serves customers.

## Turn a warning into a question

For a membership retailer, ask what would happen if renewals weakened. A useful question names a mechanism and an observation, such as tracking changes in reported renewal rates alongside fee revenue.

## Cross-check management’s discussion

Item 7 can explain whether a change in a metric was driven by volume, price, timing or another factor. The risk description tells you what could happen. The operating discussion may help you understand what happened during the period.

## Keep a dated evidence trail

Copy the section name and fiscal end date, then record the number you plan to check in the next filing. In the [research worksheet](/tools/research-template/), give the question its own row so you can revisit it.
