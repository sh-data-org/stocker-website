## Find the period on the cover

The fiscal year is the reporting year the company uses. It can end in a month other than December. Read the fiscal-year end on the cover before copying a number into a worksheet.

## Use Costco’s FY2026 as an example

Costco’s FY2026 ended August 30, 2026. Its FY2025 ended August 31, 2025. Both cover 52 weeks, even though the end dates differ. A September-to-August-style year should not be treated as January through December.

## Check the length as well as the date

Some retail calendars include a 53rd week in certain years. Look for the number of weeks above the statement and read management’s discussion for any effect. The filing date can be weeks later than the reporting period end.

## Write the label beside the number

Record the fiscal label, ending date, number of weeks and whether the number is annual or quarterly. When comparing two companies, keep those details visible. Use the [stock research guide](/learn/how-to-research-stocks/) for the next step.
