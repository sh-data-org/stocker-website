## What happened at Nvidia?

Nvidia announced on November 12 that co-founder Jensen Huang had resigned as president, CEO and board member. Its SEC filing says the board accepted the resignation on November 11. CFO Colette Kress has taken over as interim CEO and will continue as CFO. Nvidia is scheduled to report results on November 17 around 4:20 p.m. Eastern, followed by its earnings call at 5 p.m. Eastern. [Nvidia’s transition announcement](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/default.aspx) · [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm)

## Why are chip exports under scrutiny?

On November 8, Nvidia’s board announced an independent review after reports that Huang had been targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. At the time, the board said it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets had been compromised. On November 11, senators Elizabeth Warren and Chris Coons asked federal officials to preserve records related to Nvidia chip exports and export-control decisions. A board investigation and a records request establish that scrutiny is underway. The business question is whether it leads to a new restriction, a compliance cost or a change in guidance. [Nvidia board statement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors?embed=true) · [Senate letter](https://www.banking.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/20261111_warren_coons_letter_to_wh_commerce_state_re_nvidia_record_retentionpdf.pdf)

## Three things to check on November 17

1. **Succession.** Does the board give a timeline for choosing a permanent CEO? Check whether Kress discusses changes to product plans, spending or oversight while she holds both roles.
2. **Export exposure.** Look for new information on which products can ship, what requires a license and how management accounts for uncertainty in its outlook. In the July quarter, Nvidia reported about **$7.9 billion of revenue from customers headquartered in China, including Hong Kong**, against **$96.2 billion in total revenue**. That geography is a starting point for reading the filing. The revenue impact of any new rule depends on the products and transactions the rule covers. [Nvidia quarterly filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)
3. **Data Center growth and margins.** Nvidia reported about **$89.0 billion in Data Center revenue** last quarter. Compare the new figure and gross margin with the prior quarter, then read the next-quarter guidance. The results show what customers bought during the quarter. Guidance shows what management expects after the leadership change. [Nvidia segment disclosure](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/R20.htm)

Before the call, write down one disclosure that would change how you read Nvidia’s outlook. Afterward, check the earnings release and filing for that specific item.
