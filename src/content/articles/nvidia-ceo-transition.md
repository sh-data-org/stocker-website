## What happened at Nvidia?

Nvidia announced on November 12 that co-founder Jensen Huang had resigned as president, CEO and board member. Its SEC filing says the board accepted the resignation on November 11. CFO Colette Kress has taken over as interim CEO and will continue as CFO. Nvidia subsequently reported its fiscal third-quarter results on November 17. This update uses those results as the starting point for the next research decision. [Nvidia’s transition announcement](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/default.aspx) · [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000082/nvda-20261111.htm)

## Why are chip exports under scrutiny?

On November 8, Nvidia’s board announced an independent review after reports that Huang had been targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. At the time, the board said it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets had been compromised. On November 11, senators Elizabeth Warren and Chris Coons asked federal officials to preserve records related to Nvidia chip exports and export-control decisions. A board investigation and a records request establish that scrutiny is underway. The business question is whether it leads to a new restriction, a compliance cost or a change in guidance. [Nvidia board statement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors?embed=true) · [Senate letter](https://www.banking.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/20261111_warren_coons_letter_to_wh_commerce_state_re_nvidia_record_retentionpdf.pdf)

## What Nvidia reported on November 17

For the quarter ended October 25, Nvidia reported **$115.8 billion in revenue**, **$107.6 billion in Data Center revenue** and a **74.8% GAAP gross margin**. Its Q4 revenue outlook is **$130 billion, plus or minus 3%**, with a 71%–72% gross margin range. Q4 includes an extra week, and the outlook assumes no Data Center compute revenue from China. Keep those assumptions alongside the figures when comparing periods. [Nvidia’s Q3 results and outlook](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)
