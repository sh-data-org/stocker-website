## Net bookings, revenue and cash flow tell you different things

Take-Two uses **net bookings** to track sales activity across its business, including digitally sold products and physical products sold into distribution. GAAP revenue follows accounting rules about when sales are recognized, and operating cash flow reflects cash collected and paid during the period. A strong launch can lift bookings while revenue recognition and cash collections follow different schedules. Use the earnings release to check how the company explains those differences.
