## Find the numbers in two places

Net income is profit after recognized expenses and taxes. Operating cash flow shows cash generated or used by operating activities. Costco reported $9.226 billion of net income and $15.817 billion of operating cash flow for FY2026.

## Read the bridge between them

Start at the top of the operating section of the cash-flow statement. A company using the indirect method starts with net income and adjusts for noncash items, such as depreciation, and changes in operating assets and liabilities.

## Check changes in working capital

Inventory, receivables and accounts payable affect cash timing. A company can sell inventory it already paid for or delay cash outflows through the timing of payments. Compare the line items and management’s explanation before treating a large gap as permanent.

## Record both figures and one reason

Use the same reporting period and currency for both figures. In the [worksheet](/tools/research-template/), record which adjustment changed the comparison and whether it recurs.
