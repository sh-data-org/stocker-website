## Original recommendation

Build a small product website with an educational library about understanding company data and researching stocks. Combine **articles with measurable demand** and **product/comparison pages with a direct reason to install**. Use Stocker's real screens as the bridge between the two.

The first release should have the homepage, discovery and watchlist feature pages, trust/support pages, and two substantive articles. Expand to six articles, inspect indexing and conversion, then choose the next six. The 24 opportunities below are a prioritized backlog, not an instruction to publish all 24.

Suggested positioning: **Discover companies. Understand their numbers. Build your watchlist.**

The current public listing confirms discovery based on interests, saved stocks, charts, company profiles, valuation metrics, financials, dividends, and news. Stocker is free to download with Premium purchases and is a research product rather than a broker. Base website claims on this current listing and the shipping app, not old marketing material or unreleased repository features. ## What DataForSEO actually measured

- Used the signed-in **Gaver Apps DataForSEO Playground**, through the existing Chrome session.
- Keyword Overview: **60 submitted phrases; 48 returned records; 47 with numeric volume**. Twelve phrases were absent and one returned null volume. Missing means unknown, not zero.
- Settings: Google, United States **2840**, English **en**; no clickstream add-on.
- Keyword records were last updated between **17 September and 20 October 2026**. These are database estimates retrieved today, not November actual search counts.
- Six live Google mobile searches checked intent and current competition: negative P/E, revenue vs earnings, sector vs industry, best stock watchlist app, Finviz alternative, and Tinder for stocks.
- Two 50-result Keyword Ideas requests produced mostly broad ticker/quote searches. Those results were excluded from the content shortlist because they did not fit the product or editorial intent.
- API response costs totalled **$0.06576**, about 7 cents, against the existing balance. No funds were added.
- [Measured keyword CSV](stocker-dataforseo-keywords.csv) contains all 60 seeds and explicitly marks missing data. [Research log](stocker-research-log.md) records request IDs and live-search observations.

**How to read the numbers:** volume is estimated average monthly US searches, not visits, unique people, iPhone users, or installs. Organic keyword difficulty (KD) is a 0–100, backlink-based proxy; it is not Google Ads competition and not a ranking probability. A zero does not make an established finance result easy to outrank. Do not sum close variants to invent total addressable traffic. [Volume definition](https://dataforseo.com/help-center/what-is-search-volume), [KD methodology](https://dataforseo.com/help-center/what-is-keyword-difficulty-and-how-is-it-calculated)

Recent monthly values reinforce the need for caution: negative P/E averaged 1,000 but September was 590; revenue vs earnings averaged 1,900 but September was 1,300; best stock watchlist app averaged 70 but September was 40. The CSV preserves the reported averages, not the most recent month's count.

## Briefs for the first six articles

### 1. How to research stocks

- **Answer:** explain the research sequence immediately: understand the business, inspect its results and valuation, identify risks, record questions, revisit evidence.
- **Sections:** company profile; revenue and earnings; valuation in context; dividends when relevant; primary filings and risks; a reusable checklist.
- **Original asset:** annotate real shipping-app screens, with dates. Include a free printable checklist on the page.
- **Sources:** company investor-relations material and the [SEC financial-statements guide](https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/begfinstmtguide.htm).
- **CTA:** **Find a company to research in Stocker.** The app helps organize research; a brief checklist does not replace full due diligence.
- **Links:** negative P/E, revenue/earnings, price targets, watchlist feature page.

### 2. Negative P/E

- **Answer:** negative earnings can produce a negative mathematical ratio; many providers show N/A instead. Neither automatically identifies an undervalued stock.
- **Sections:** calculation; negative vs unavailable; why losses happen; which period the data covers; what other information to examine.
- **Original asset:** a small, labelled fictional example plus an actual app screen only if the displayed basis and missing-value behavior are verified.
- **Source:** [Schwab P/E explanation](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/stock-analysis-using-pe-ratio).
- **CTA:** **Look beyond one ratio—explore the company's financials in Stocker.**
- **Links:** revenue/earnings, trailing/forward P/E, research checklist.

### 3. Sector vs industry

- **Answer:** show a broad sector and a narrower industry, distinguishing classification systems and provider labels.
- **Sections:** hierarchy; worked company examples; why same-sector businesses can differ; using sectors to explore rather than to assume diversification.
- **Original asset:** simple hierarchy diagram and the real Stocker sector chooser.
- **Source:** [MSCI GICS overview](https://www.msci.com/indexes/index-resources/gics). Verify Stocker's provider taxonomy rather than calling it GICS without evidence.
- **CTA:** **Choose your interests and discover companies in Stocker.**
- **Links:** discovery feature page, company research guide, later sector hub.

### 4. Stock price targets

- **Answer:** a target is an estimate tied to assumptions and a horizon, not a guaranteed future price.
- **Sections:** individual vs consensus targets; date and forecast horizon; revisions; disagreement; comparison with business fundamentals.
- **Original asset:** annotated display using dated, sourced data or explicitly fictional numbers. Explain where Stocker's figure comes from before publication.
- **CTA:** **Review the target alongside the company's financials.**
- **Links:** research checklist, EPS, negative P/E.

### 5. Revenue vs earnings

- **Answer:** revenue measures sales; earnings reflect the result after relevant expenses. Define the exact earnings measure being discussed.
- **Sections:** simple income-statement bridge; growing sales with falling profit; annual vs quarterly periods; why cash flow is separate; where to find the figures.
- **Original asset:** a fictional income statement plus actual Stocker financial-chart screenshots.
- **Source:** [SEC financial-statements guide](https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/begfinstmtguide.htm).
- **CTA:** **Explore annual and quarterly company results in Stocker.**
- **Links:** EPS, negative P/E, company research guide.

### 6. Trailing vs forward P/E

- **Answer:** distinguish historical earnings from forecasts, using a consistent share price and clearly labelled periods.
- **Sections:** two calculations; reasons they differ; analyst-estimate uncertainty; negative earnings; apples-to-apples comparisons.
- **Original asset:** calculation table and labels showing exactly which data Stocker provides. Do not invent a forward-P/E app feature.
- **CTA:** **Understand the numbers behind the companies you save.**
- **Links:** negative P/E, company research guide, EPS.

## Page experience, crawling and credibility

- Serve complete indexable HTML with unique titles, descriptions, one main heading, self-consistent canonical URLs and crawlable internal links.
- Provide robots.txt and an XML sitemap of canonical public pages. Exclude internal search/filter combinations and thin archives from the index strategy.
- Use stable URLs, meaningful 404s and redirects only for real replacements. Keep staging out of search.
- Link each article from its hub and to the most relevant feature page and two or three useful related guides. Backfill links on existing articles.
- Compress screenshots, reserve image dimensions, load demonstrations efficiently and avoid blocking the initial text with heavy scripts. Aim for LCP ≤2.5 seconds, INP <200 ms and CLS <0.1, assessed on real mobile experience as data becomes available. [Google Core Web Vitals](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals)
- Use visible, truthful Organization, Article and Breadcrumb information. Consider SoftwareApplication markup on the product page; validate it and satisfy Google's required fields before expecting eligibility. Never invent ratings or reviews to qualify. [Google app markup](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/software-app)
- Give every finance article a real author, publication/update dates, primary sources, clear examples and appropriate factual review. Make the site's commercial relationship to Stocker obvious. Disclaimers do not replace accurate content or competent review. [Google helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)
- Answer questions directly and include useful tables and original visuals. These help readers and potential AI citations; Google states that its AI search features do not require a separate special optimization scheme. [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

## Turn visits into installs

**Search → useful answer → relevant Stocker screen/workflow → App Store → first saved company.**

- Give the full answer before asking for installation. Show the relevant screen beside the explanation, not an unrelated generic app banner.
- Put one contextual CTA after the useful worked example and a second at the article end. A compact mobile download bar is a test candidate, provided it does not obstruct reading.
- On iPhone/iPad, link directly to the app's real store listing. On desktop, offer a QR code leading through a tracked campaign URL. Do not imply Android availability without checking the current distribution.
- Explain **free to download, Premium available**; verify precise limits and current offers before making numerical claims. Keep screenshots and App Store expectations consistent.
- Begin with the current App Store page. Later test custom product pages for discovery, watchlists or research only if there is enough measurable traffic and matching screenshots.
- Do not promise a clicked stock or saved web watchlist will survive installation unless deferred routing has been implemented and tested.

## Measurement that separates clicks from installs

| Stage              | Measure                                                                                 | What it establishes                                                                                   |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Visibility         | Search Console indexed pages, non-brand queries, impressions, clicks and country/device | Search exposure and visits; not installs.                                                             |
| Website engagement | Organic landing sessions, template downloads, contextual CTA clicks, QR route visits    | Whether a page attracts useful interest.                                                              |
| Store conversion   | App Store Connect campaign product-page views and first-time downloads                  | Aggregate attributed downloads where Apple reports them.                                              |
| Activation         | Onboarding completion and first saved company in existing app analytics                 | Product use; only call this SEO-attributed where a validated attribution mechanism supports the join. |
| Value              | Trial/purchase and retention by supported campaign/cohort reporting                     | Whether acquired users become valuable, subject to reporting limits.                                  |

Generate real App Store Connect campaign links with the account's provider token and a campaign token. Start with a few content-cluster campaigns such as research, metrics and comparisons; keep article-level CTA IDs in web analytics. Apple's thresholds can hide small campaigns, so one token per tiny article would fragment reporting. Apple documents a delay of at least 24 hours and minimum reporting counts; missing data must not be recorded as zero installs. [Apple campaign links](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/campaign-links)

UTM parameters alone do not automatically connect a web visitor to an iOS first open. Existing Firebase/Amplitude instrumentation is useful for activation, but the website-to-app attribution path still needs implementation and validation.

Illustrative arithmetic, **not a forecast**: 1,000 eligible iPhone visits × 5% store-click rate × 30% store conversion = 15 downloads. Actual rates and search traffic are unknown. Estimate economics from measured data after launch rather than promising installs from keyword volume.

## 90-day rollout

### Days 1–14: establish the site

- Confirm domain, target market and old-domain status.
- Build the core product/trust pages and publish articles 1 and 2 with real screenshots.
- Verify crawlability, mobile layout, store links, campaign parameters and web events; submit the sitemap and inspect representative URLs.
- Keep source code work on the required development branch and show the actual result before any publication approval.

### Days 15–30: complete the initial test

- Publish articles 3–6, at most two substantial articles per week.
- Link the cluster together and check indexing/queries weekly.
- Reuse approved article visuals in Stocker's existing content workflow. No social posting or outreach is authorized by this research plan alone.
- Judge this phase on technical readiness and early demand signals, not a guaranteed traffic target.

### Days 31–60: expand what earns attention

- Select up to six B articles using observed search queries, indexation and useful CTA activity.
- Test a watchlist-app comparison only after checking competitor features hands-on.
- Add the worksheet/template if visitors show demand. A functioning useful resource is preferable to a thin download landing page.
- Update owned App Store marketing/support links and brand profiles after the domain is live and changes are authorized. Identify relevant education/newsletter partners for later outreach.

### Days 61–90: improve conversion and consolidate

- Refresh promising pages, improve titles with weak CTR at comparable positions and strengthen relevant CTAs.
- If indexed pages have little exposure, reassess intent and competition before increasing publishing volume.
- If pages attract visits but few store clicks, revise the product bridge. If store clicks do not convert, examine the listing/offer match.
- Compare cohorts over sufficiently long windows; do not change direction based on a handful of downloads.
- Expand into company comparisons or more advanced financial subjects only when catalog coverage, sources and maintenance capacity are established.

## What remains open

Domain, final country/language, old-domain control, current US storefront details, live catalog coverage and some displayed financial-field definitions still need confirmation before implementation/publication. Rankings, traffic, install conversion and a specific time to SEO traction cannot be promised from this dataset.

**Immediate decision:** once the domain is chosen, build the core website and the first two articles, with measurement ready on launch day. The initial six-article cluster is the first experiment; the backlog supplies options for what follows.
